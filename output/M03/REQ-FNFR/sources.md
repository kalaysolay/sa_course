## Source Boundary

External research is disabled for this topic. The lesson stays at beginner level and focuses on practical recognition and formulation of functional and nonfunctional requirements.

## Usable Teaching Points

1. Functional requirements describe system behavior and functions.
2. Nonfunctional requirements describe quality attributes, constraints and operating conditions.
3. The same feature often needs both functional and nonfunctional requirements.
4. Nonfunctional requirements should be made as measurable and testable as possible.
5. Classification matters less than preserving meaning and verification.
