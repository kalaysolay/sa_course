## Usable Teaching Points

1. A requirement is a fixed agreement about a need, behavior, property or constraint.
2. Requirements connect business need, system behavior and acceptance/testing.
3. Requirements exist at multiple levels: business, user and system.
4. Requirements can describe data, business rules, constraints and acceptance criteria.
5. The next lesson narrows the distinction between functional and nonfunctional requirements.
