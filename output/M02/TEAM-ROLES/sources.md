External research is disabled for this topic. The lesson explains project roles at beginner level and does not import a full project management or agile certification framework.

## Usable Teaching Points

1. Role and job title are different: one person may perform several roles, and responsibilities differ by company.
2. Analyst work happens inside a team: product, business, design, development, QA, architecture, security, operations and stakeholders all influence clarity.
3. The analyst should identify who knows, who decides, who implements, who checks and who accepts.
4. Compliance examples should show both business users and technical participants.
5. The lesson should prepare learners for development process models in the next topic.
