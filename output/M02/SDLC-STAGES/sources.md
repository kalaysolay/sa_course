## Source Boundary

External research is disabled for this topic. The lesson explains SDLC at beginner level and does not go deep into DevOps, operations, release engineering or formal process frameworks.

## Usable Teaching Points

1. SDLC is a map of product work from idea to operation and change, not always a strict waterfall sequence.
2. The analyst's questions change by stage: problem, context, requirements, design, implementation clarifications, testing, acceptance, release and support.
3. Requirements appear, evolve and are checked across the whole lifecycle.
4. Compliance examples should connect stages to requests, gifts, declarations, dashboards, SSO, roles and statuses.
5. The lesson should close module 2 and prepare learners for module 3 on requirements.
