## Source Boundary

External research is disabled for this topic. The lesson stays at overview level: it does not teach Scrum, Kanban or project management deeply and uses process models only to explain how they affect analytical work.

## Usable Teaching Points

1. Development model defines the rhythm from idea to working result.
2. Sequential work emphasizes upfront clarity and controlled change.
3. Iterative and incremental work delivers product in parts and learns from feedback.
4. Agile does not remove analysis or documentation; it changes timing, size and update rhythm.
5. Scrum-like work needs backlog readiness, acceptance criteria and analysis ahead of sprint planning.
6. Kanban-like work needs flow visibility, WIP limits and attention to blocked analysis.
7. Definition of Ready and Definition of Done help align expectations.
