## Usable Teaching Points

1. Analytical competence is not a set of isolated talents. Communication, structuring, critical thinking, modeling, writing and uncertainty management work together in one cycle.
2. Good questions clarify problem, users, context, constraints, terms, examples and decision rules. Poor questions jump straight to an implementation detail before the problem is understood.
3. Structuring turns messy notes into categories: facts, assumptions, open questions, decisions, constraints and next actions.
4. Critical thinking means checking what is known, what is assumed and what must be verified with a stakeholder, example, artifact or team member.
5. Basic modeling is not "drawing for beauty"; a simple process or data sketch exposes missing actors, statuses, transitions, fields and ownership.
6. Written fixation protects agreement. A short message after a meeting can be more valuable than a large document that nobody confirmed.
7. Uncertainty is normal in analysis. The professional move is to make uncertainty visible, reduce it step by step and avoid inventing facts.

## Compliance Examples To Reuse

- Raw request: "Employees want to see the status of their compliance requests."
- Possible users: employee, compliance officer, head of compliance, product owner, developer, QA.
- Terms to verify: request status, clarification, closed request, internal comment, declaration, gift registration.
- Simple structure: what employee sees, what officer does, what head of compliance measures, what the system must store.
- Negative boundary: this is an internal company Compliance system, not KYC and not an external customer onboarding system.
