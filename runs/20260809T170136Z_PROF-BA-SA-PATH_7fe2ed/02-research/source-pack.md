## Source Boundary

External research is disabled for this topic. The lecture should not import a large external methodology or competency framework. It should explain the course's own path: from business request to problem framing, users and processes, requirements, models, data, interfaces and specification.

## Usable Teaching Points

1. A business problem usually arrives as a rough phrase, not as a neat requirement. The analyst's job is to turn "make it convenient" into a checked task.
2. The path is not a factory conveyor. It is closer to a map: problem, stakeholders, process, requirements, system behavior, data, interfaces, specification and acceptance.
3. BA and SA are useful lenses, not sealed rooms. In this course the learner tries both: understanding why the business needs a change and describing how the system should support it.
4. Compliance project route: requests, gifts, declarations, officer workspace, employee visibility, dashboards, SSO and permissions.
5. A friendly conference style is acceptable for this lesson: concrete examples, occasional light jokes, restrained cultural references, no meme overload.

## Compliance Thread

Start from a raw request: "Сотрудникам неудобно работать с обращениями в комплаенс: они не понимают статус и часто получают уточняющие вопросы".

Possible path:

1. Clarify the business problem and value.
2. Identify users and scenarios.
3. Describe the current and target process.
4. Formulate requirements and acceptance criteria.
5. Move toward models: statuses, state transitions, data fields, roles, visibility rules and interfaces.
6. Assemble a system specification that development and QA can use.
